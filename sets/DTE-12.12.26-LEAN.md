## Key route · Two Waves (her arc)

**hold** = stay in key · **up** = lift (clockwise) · **down** = release (counter-clockwise, relative or mood change) · **colour** = same energy, new mood (relative, mood change) · **land** = hold or ease down for the next DJ.
**Blends** (long overlap, layers OK): = hold · ↔ relative (same notes, new mood) · ↕ mood change (same root, minor↔major) · ↑ lift · ↓ release / step down · ↓ pressure shift (down while energy rises: darker, heavier) · ⤡ diagonal.
**Swaps** (8-16 bars over a break, outgoing stripped to drums): ⇈ whole-tone or minor-third lift · ⇊ whole-tone or minor-third drop.
**Cuts** (on a breakdown, or Key Shift ±1 to blend): ⤴ semitone lift · ⤵ semitone drop · !! key jump. The Note column says how to play each swap and cut.
**Lifts** = up-steps spent in the phase vs its budget: save them for the peak.
